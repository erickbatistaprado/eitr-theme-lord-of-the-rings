# Lord of the Rings artwork

The six wallpapers are fan art inspired by J. R. R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings, shared
here for personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist; the Tolkien
works and their films belong to their rights holders. This theme is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any of them.

- `01-the-journey.jpg`: the Shire, Orthanc and Mount Doom; artist unknown
- `02-the-one-ring.jpg`: the One Ring's inscription; artist unknown
- `03-doors-of-durin.jpg`: the Doors of Durin; artist unknown
- `04-balrog.jpg`: the Balrog; artist unknown
- `05-minas-morgul.jpg`: Minas Morgul; artist unknown
- `06-you-shall-not-pass.jpg`: Gandalf and the Balrog, by Anato Finnstark

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: Middle-earth night background, parchment text,
the One Ring's gold as accent, Balrog fire red, Shire green, misty blue, wizard violet and
Morgul green.
